# The Human Brain

## Overview

The brain and spinal cord form the **central nervous system**. The brain helps process sensory information, coordinate movement, form memories, and regulate essential body functions.

Different regions contribute to different tasks, but they work together rather than acting as completely independent control boxes.

## A few important regions

| Region | Examples of its roles |
| --- | --- |
| Cerebral cortex | Sensory processing and higher functions |
| Cerebellum | Balance, coordination, and learning motor tasks |
| Brainstem | Communication with the spinal cord and functions such as breathing |
| Hypothalamus | Temperature regulation and hormonal control |
| Hippocampus | Memory formation |

This is a simplified overview.

## Communication between cells

**Neurons** are specialized cells that send signals. Many communicate across small gaps called synapses. The receiving cell's response depends on the signals it receives and how they are combined.

## Think about it

Catching a ball involves seeing its motion, planning a response, coordinating muscles, and adjusting balance. Many parts of the nervous system participate in this apparently simple action.
