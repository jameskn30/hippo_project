# The Water Cycle

## Overview

The water cycle describes how water moves between the atmosphere, Earth's surface, and underground storage. Water can be a liquid, solid, or gas. **Sunlight and gravity** help drive its movement.

The cycle has no single starting point. Water may travel quickly through a river or remain stored for much longer in ice or underground.

## Common processes

- **Evaporation:** liquid water becomes water vapor.
- **Transpiration:** plants release water vapor.
- **Condensation:** water vapor forms liquid droplets.
- **Precipitation:** water falls as rain, snow, or other forms.
- **Runoff:** water travels over land into streams and rivers.
- **Infiltration:** water enters soil and can move toward groundwater.

## Follow one possible journey

1. Sunlight helps water evaporate from the ocean.
2. Water vapor condenses into cloud droplets.
3. Rain falls over land.
4. Water flows into a river or seeps underground.
5. Some eventually returns to the ocean.
