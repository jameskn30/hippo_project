# The Solar System

## Overview

The solar system includes the Sun and the objects that orbit it: planets, moons, dwarf planets, asteroids, and comets. It formed about **4.6 billion years ago** from a collapsing cloud of gas and dust. Most of that material gathered into the Sun; smaller clumps became other bodies.

## The eight planets

Listed from nearest to farthest from the Sun:

1. Mercury
2. Venus
3. Earth
4. Mars
5. Jupiter
6. Saturn
7. Uranus
8. Neptune

| Group | Members | Main characteristic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terrestrial planets | Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars | Rocky surfaces |
| Gas giants | Jupiter, Saturn | Large planets dominated by gas |
| Ice giants | Uranus, Neptune | Different composition from the gas giants |

## Beyond the planets

The asteroid belt lies between Mars and Jupiter. Beyond Neptune, the **Kuiper Belt** contains icy objects, including Pluto. The more distant Oort Cloud is inferred from observations and models; it has not been directly observed.

## Remember

Pluto is a dwarf planet. The solar system extends far beyond the orbit of Neptune.
