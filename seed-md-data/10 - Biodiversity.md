# Biodiversity

## Overview

**Biodiversity** means the variety of life and the patterns formed by living things. It includes differences within species, between species, and among ecosystems.

Looking only at the number of species misses important parts of the picture. Genetic variation and relationships between organisms also matter.

## Three levels

| Level | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Genetic diversity | Differences among varieties of a crop |
| Species diversity | Different plants, animals, and fungi in a forest |
| Ecosystem diversity | Forests, wetlands, grasslands, and coral reefs |

## Why it matters

Living communities contribute to:

- Pollination of plants, including crops
- Decomposition and nutrient cycling
- Water purification
- Food, materials, and genetic resources
- Recreation and cultural experiences

These benefits depend on interactions among organisms and their surroundings.

## Threats to biodiversity

Habitat loss, unsustainable harvesting, pollution, invasive species, and climate change can alter those communities. Protecting biodiversity involves both living organisms and the habitats that support them.

## Remember

An ecosystem is more than a collection of species names. Its relationships are part of what makes it function.
