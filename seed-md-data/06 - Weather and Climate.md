# Weather and Climate

## Overview

Weather and climate both describe the atmosphere, but over different timescales. **Weather** is the condition at a particular place and time. **Climate** describes patterns over a much longer period.

## Compare them

| Question | Weather or climate? |
| --- | --- |
| Is it raining this afternoon? | Weather |
| Is January usually cold here? | Climate |
| How windy is it right now? | Weather |
| How much rain does this region usually receive? | Climate |

Weather can change quickly. Climate descriptions often use observations collected over **30 years**, including temperature, rainfall, humidity, and wind.

## How observations become patterns

1. Instruments record conditions at many locations.
2. Observations accumulate over days, seasons, and years.
3. Scientists calculate averages and examine variation.
4. Long-term records help reveal trends and compare unusual events with typical conditions.

## Remember

A single cold day does not establish a climate trend. Climate is understood through repeated observations over time.

**Global warming** refers to rising average temperatures; **climate change** includes broader changes in the climate system.
