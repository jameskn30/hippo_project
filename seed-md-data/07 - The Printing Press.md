# The Printing Press

## Overview

Printing with movable type uses separate characters that can be arranged into text, printed, and reused. Johannes Gutenberg developed a major European system of printing with **movable metal type** in the fifteenth century.

His work was important, but he did **not invent printing itself**. Printing traditions and movable type existed in Asia before Gutenberg's European work.

## The Gutenberg Bible

The Gutenberg Bible was produced in the mid-1450s and is a landmark of early European printing. Its carefully designed Gothic lettering and page layout show that printing was also a craft.

Some finishing work, including decorative details, was still performed by hand. Early printed books therefore combined new printing methods with manuscript traditions.

## Why reusable type mattered

- Individual characters could be assembled into many different pages.
- A prepared page could produce multiple copies.
- Type could be reused for later text.

## Remember

The history of printing spans several cultures. Gutenberg's achievement belongs within that longer history, rather than marking the first time anyone printed text.
